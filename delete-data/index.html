Delete your Rosin data

This page is for Rosin: Violin Practice, the Android app by Layam Labs. Without an account, your data is in two places: on your phone, and the anonymous usage statistics the app sends us. If you sign in with Google, there is a third: your account data, which includes the class data and any recordings you shared with a teacher.

1. Your account (only if you signed in)

To delete your account:

  • In Rosin, open Settings, then Account, then Delete account.
  • If you no longer have the app, go to https://rosin-accounts.layamlabs.workers.dev/delete-account and sign in with the same Google account.

Deleting the account removes at once the account, its profiles, your backup, your practice summaries, your recordings, the comments and homework addressed to you, your class places, and classes you run alone. We keep no copy. If a recording could not be deleted from storage at that moment, we retry daily until it is. Practice saved on your phone stays there until you erase it (section 2).

You can also delete part of it without deleting the account:

  • A recording you shared with a teacher: open Settings, then Shared with your teacher, and delete it. The copy on our server is deleted at once.
  • A class: leave it in the app. Your teacher stops seeing anything of yours at once, and we delete the rest 30 days later. A parent can do this for a child, or delete the child's profile.

A teacher is a person, not part of Layam Labs. If a teacher has already played a recording or read a summary, we cannot undo that.

2. Data on your phone

Your practice history, scores, streaks, settings, saved scans and recordings of your runs are stored on your phone. To delete all of it:

  1. Open Android Settings, then Apps, then Rosin.
  2. Tap Storage, then Clear storage. Or uninstall the app.

This takes effect at once. We hold no copy of this data unless you signed in (section 1).

3. Usage statistics and bug reports we received

If usage statistics were on, the app sent us anonymous events (which screens were opened, which features were used, error reports) tagged with a random ID. They never include your name, email, location or any recording of your playing. To delete them:

  1. In Rosin, open Settings, then Privacy, and tap Copy next to your Anonymous ID.
  2. Email info@layamlabs.com with the subject "Delete my Rosin data" and paste the ID.
  3. We confirm within 7 days and delete every event and bug report linked to that ID within 30 days.

To stop sending statistics from now on, turn off Share usage data & bug reports in the same Privacy section.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your account data (section 1), and all usage events and bug reports linked to your Anonymous ID.
  • Kept: nothing that is linked to you. Totals we have already counted (for example, how many people opened the tuner in a week) carry no ID and cannot be traced back to you.
  • If you uninstall the app before copying your ID, we cannot find your events, because they hold nothing else that identifies you.

More detail is in the Privacy Policy, section 11.
