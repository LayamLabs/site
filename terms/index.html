5. Music content in the app

5.1 What we ship

The catalogue built into the app is drawn from works whose composition is in the public domain, and every piece carries a manifest recording its source and its licence. As of the date above, all of the over 180 catalogued pieces are recorded as public domain. The sources are:

  • Editions uploaded by users to MuseScore.com, with the uploader credited in each manifest and the source URL and file hash recorded.
  • Files from the PDMX dataset.
  • One score derived from a PDF on IMSLP (Wohlfahrt Op. 45, Schirmer 1905).
  • Optical music recognition output derived from the above.

Composers in the catalogue include Kayser, Wohlfahrt and others who died well over a century ago, so the underlying compositions are out of copyright. Where a modern engraving or edition was the source, the uploader or editor is credited in that piece's manifest.
